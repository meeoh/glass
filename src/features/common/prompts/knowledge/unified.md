## UNIFIED COMMERCE (D2C + RETAIL) — PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

### The core narrative
"Shopify started with e-commerce — that's been the core for 12+ years. Then we built POS to unify in-store and online. One inventory, one customer record, one analytics dashboard — whether the sale happens online, in-store, or anywhere."

### The core pain to surface
Most merchants run online on one platform and in-store on another (Retail Pro, Square, Lightspeed, Oracle). Three systems, three customer databases, three inventory ledgers. A customer who buys online and returns in-store is invisible. Multiple MIDs = manual reconciliation.

### Diagnostic questions (run on every call with physical retail)
- "What POS system are you running — is it the same as your online store?"
- "Each of your stores — do they have their own MID and bank account, or is it centralized?"
- "If a customer bought online last week and walked in tomorrow, would your till staff see that purchase?"
- "When you look at total customer LTV, are you joining in-store and online spend — or are they two separate numbers?"

### The network effect
"When a customer taps their card in-store, if they've ever shopped on any Shopify store — not just yours — their profile populates automatically. 250M+ Shop Pay users. Your new in-store customer might already be in the Shopify ecosystem. No standalone POS can do that."

### Key proof points
- JB Hi-Fi (Australia): 200+ retail locations migrated to Shopify POS in under 6 months
- Allbirds: all D2C, retail stores, pop-ups, and wholesale from a single Plus account
- Gymshark: POS for brand pop-ups and experiential retail — same customer profile as online
- Culture Kings (Australia): streetwear, online + physical stores unified

### When to position Unified
Surface this when merchant mentions: retail locations, POS systems, in-store, pop-ups, omni-channel, multiple MIDs, manual reconciliation, or names Retail Pro / Sin 7 / Square / Lightspeed / Oracle.

### Plus inclusion
"When you license Shopify Plus, POS is included in the licence — not a separate purchase. Unified commerce without separate licensing."
