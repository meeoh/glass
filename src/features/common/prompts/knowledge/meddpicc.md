## MEDDPICC QUALIFICATION FRAMEWORK

### M — Business Impact (Metrics)
Key questions to assess:
- Have you identified and quantified the key business metrics that matter to this prospect?'
- Is there a clear baseline to measure improvement against?'
- Have you tied Shopify's value to specific financial outcomes (revenue, cost savings, efficiency)?
- Have they calculated or validated ROI / payback period?'
- Are the metrics linked to a compelling event or urgency?'
- Do stakeholders agree on what success looks like?'
- Are the metrics part of their written success criteria?'

### E — Decision Maker (Economic Buyer)
Key questions to assess:
- Have you identified who has final budget authority for this deal?'
- Have you had a direct conversation with the economic buyer?'
- Does the EB understand the business case and value proposition?'
- Is the EB actively engaged and supportive of moving forward?'
- Have you identified any risks or concerns the EB has?'
- Is there a plan to get the EB's formal sign-off at the right time?

### D — Their Criteria (Decision Criteria)
Key questions to assess:
- Have you uncovered all formal and informal evaluation criteria?'
- Do you know how they are weighting each criterion?'
- Have you mapped Shopify's strengths to their top criteria?
- Are there criteria that could disadvantage Shopify, and have you addressed them?'
- Has the prospect confirmed that Shopify meets their must-have requirements?'
- Do you know who owns each criterion in the evaluation?'
- Are the criteria aligned with what the economic buyer cares about?'

### D — Their Process (Decision Process)
Key questions to assess:
- Do you know all steps in their decision-making process?'
- Have you mapped out all stakeholders and decision-makers involved?'
- Do you understand the timeline and key milestones to a decision?'
- Have you identified any internal governance or committee approvals required?'
- Do you know who the final decision-maker is (vs. the recommender)?'
- Is there a pilot or proof-of-concept stage that needs to happen?'
- Are there competing priorities or initiatives that could delay their decision?'

### P — Paperwork & Legal (Paper Process)
Key questions to assess:
- Do you know what legal and procurement steps are required to close?'
- Have you engaged their procurement or legal team early enough?'
- Are you aware of any standard contract requirements or deviations they need?'
- Do you know their typical contract review timeline?'
- Are there security reviews, InfoSec, or data privacy assessments needed?'
- Have you validated that budget is formally allocated or can be obtained?'
- Is there a single owner on their side driving the paper process?'

### I — Their Pain (Identified Pain)
Key questions to assess:
- Have you clearly identified the prospect's primary business pain?
- Is the pain explicit (stated) or implicit (implied), and have you made it explicit?'
- Have you quantified the cost or impact of not solving this pain?'
- Is the pain felt by multiple stakeholders, not just one person?'
- Have you linked the pain to a sense of urgency or compelling event?'
- Does the EB feel and acknowledge this pain?'
- Have you confirmed that Shopify directly addresses this pain?'
- Is solving this pain a top strategic priority for the company right now?'

### C — Your Champion (Champion)
Key questions to assess:
- Have you identified an internal advocate who actively promotes Shopify?'
- Does the champion have credibility and access to the economic buyer?'
- Has the champion explicitly agreed to sell Shopify internally when you're not in the room?
- Have you provided the champion with compelling arguments and materials to use?'
- Is the champion's personal success tied to the outcome of this project?
- Have you tested the champion under pressure to validate their commitment?'
- Is the champion aware of competitive threats and prepared to counter them?'

### C — Competition (Competition)
Key questions to assess:
- Do you know which competitors are being evaluated?'
- Have you mapped Shopify's advantages vs. each competitor for this specific deal?
- Have you proactively addressed the competitor's likely talking points?
- Do you know the prospect's perception of each competitor?
- Have you identified any competitive landmines or traps to avoid?'
