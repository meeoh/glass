## FOOD, BEVERAGE & ALCOHOL VERTICAL
// Source: BigQuery analysis of 1,015 Food/Bev calls (Jan–Apr 2026)
// Key patterns: Subscription 14.3% (highest of any vertical!) | Wholesale 6.7% | ERP 10.6%

### What makes F&B merchants different
Food, beverage, and alcohol brands have unique requirements:
- **Subscription/recurring orders** are core commerce (14.3% of calls — highest of any vertical)
  → meal kits, wine clubs, coffee subscriptions, supplement boxes
- **Age verification** for alcohol merchants (21+ in US, 18+ in EU)
- **DTC vs. distributor channel** tension — most alcohol brands sell both
- **Perishable logistics** — 3PL integrations, cold chain, freshness guarantees
- **Compliance and regulations** — alcohol licensing state-by-state (US), excise duty (UK/EU)

### Subscription commerce for F&B
Subscription mentions in 14.3% of F&B calls — the primary use case.
"Shopify has native subscription capability through Selling Plans, plus the world's largest
subscription app ecosystem (Recharge, Bold, Skio). Customers can pause, skip, or swap
— all self-serve, no support tickets."

DIAGNOSTIC QUESTION: "What percentage of your revenue is subscription or recurring?
And what does your current subscription experience look like for customers?"

### The DTC vs. distributor conversation
Most F&B brands use distributors for wholesale but want a DTC channel they own.
"Marketplaces and distributors give you reach, but they own the customer relationship.
Your DTC store gives you the data, the margin, and the ability to communicate directly.
The strongest F&B brands do both — Shopify is your DTC foundation."

### Alcohol-specific coaching
- Age verification: Shopify supports age gate apps and date-of-birth verification at checkout
- State alcohol shipping compliance (US): Confirm with legal — Shopify doesn't handle compliance itself
- Alcohol regulations (UK/EU): Excise duty, labeling requirements — confirm app ecosystem support

### Key objections in F&B calls
- **"We're regulated"**: Shopify handles the commerce; compliance apps handle the verification
- **"We have complex subscription requirements"**: Shopify's Selling Plans + Recharge ecosystem
- **"Our distributors handle everything"**: DTC gives you data, margin, and direct customer relationships
- **"We need to handle 3PL/cold chain"**: Shopify integrates with major 3PLs; confirm specific requirement
