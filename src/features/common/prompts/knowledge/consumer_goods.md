## CONSUMER GOODS VERTICAL (CPG, Health Products, Household, Personal Care)
// Source: BigQuery analysis of 1,614 Consumer Goods calls (Jan–Apr 2026)
// Key patterns: Magento 6.5% (highest of any vertical) | ERP 15.9% | B2B 5.1% | Pricing 36%

### What makes Consumer Goods merchants different
Consumer Goods brands (CPG, FMCG, health products, household, personal care) are often:
- Mid-market to enterprise, with established distribution and retail channels
- Running both D2C and wholesale/B2B channels simultaneously
- Heavily integrated with ERP systems (NetSuite, SAP) for inventory and order management
- Often on Magento — the highest Magento displacement rate of any Shopify vertical (6.5%)

### ERP is a co-existing system — NOT a competitor
ERP (NetSuite, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics) handles fulfillment, inventory, and accounting.
Shopify is the commerce layer — the customer-facing storefront and checkout.
They work together, not against each other.

The right question: "What ERP are you running, and how does your current commerce 
platform connect to it today? What does that integration cost to maintain?"

- **NetSuite**: Shopify has a native connector. "Shopify handles commerce, NetSuite handles 
  the back office. The integration is native — no custom middleware."
- **SAP**: SAP Business One has a native connector. SAP S/4HANA needs solutions engineering.
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365**: Native integration available.

ERP comes up in 15.9% of Consumer Goods calls. Ask about it early — it's often where 
the hidden integration cost lives that makes the TCO argument land.

### Magento displacement — the primary Consumer Goods opportunity
Consumer Goods has the highest Magento displacement rate of any vertical (6.5% of calls).
These are often brands who built on Magento 5-7 years ago and are now carrying the weight.

Key Magento displacement questions:
- "What does your Magento agency cost annually — not just the license, but development, patches, hosting?"
- "How long does it take your team to ship a new feature or integration?"
- "What happens to your Magento stack heading into peak season — do you have confidence it holds?"

TCO proof point: Shopify platform costs average 42% lower than Magento/Adobe Commerce.
Migration speed: Heinz launched D2C in 6 weeks after deciding the risk of staying was larger.

### D2C + B2B on the same platform
Consumer Goods brands typically sell D2C (direct to consumer) AND have wholesale/trade channels.
Shopify handles both from one admin — same catalog, same inventory, separate buyer experiences.

"Your retail buyers get a B2B portal with their pricing and payment terms. Your D2C customers 
get the full Shop Pay checkout experience. One platform, one admin, one source of truth."

### Key objections in Consumer Goods calls
- **"We're on Magento and it works"** → "Works, or familiar? What does it cost your team per year 
  to keep it working — agency fees, patches, hosting, custom integrations?"
- **"Our ERP handles everything"** → "Your ERP handles fulfillment and accounting. Does it handle 
  the customer-facing storefront, checkout, and marketing? That's where Shopify lives."
- **"We sell through distributors, not direct"** → "The brands winning in your space do both. 
  D2C gives you the customer relationship, data, and margin. Shopify handles D2C + wholesale 
  from one platform."
- **"We need to integrate with [ERP]"** → "We have native connectors for NetSuite, Dynamics 365, 
  and SAP Business One. What ERP are you on? Let's confirm the integration path."

### Consumer Goods merchant proof points

- **Glossier**: rebuilt entire ecommerce on Shopify in under 3 months after leaving custom stack.

### Escalation triggers
- SAP S/4HANA or Oracle ERP integration → solutions engineering
- Complex promotional pricing (buy X get Y, tiered promotions across channels) → solutions engineering
- EDI requirements for large retail distribution partners → solutions engineering
