## SHOPIFY PAYMENTS — PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

### How it works
- Integrated payment processing — no third-party gateway needed
- Flat blended rate per transaction (same % regardless of card type: Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Rates vary by plan: Basic → Shopify → Advanced → Plus (negotiable at Plus)
- No monthly gateway fees, no setup fees
- Payouts: daily (US/CA: 2 business day delay; UK/EU: 3 days; AU/NZ: 3-4 days)
- Payout currency matches store currency — no forced FX conversion

### Rate objection handling
- NEVER promise specific rates on a call — "we have a rate review process for high-volume merchants"
- Blended rate = same % regardless of card type. NOT interchange++ (variable by card network)
- When merchant compares to interchange++: "Interchange++ looks cheaper on paper because the headline rate is lower — but it passes the full card network cost to you. Amex is different from Visa Debit. Your actual rate varies every month. Shopify Payments is blended — same rate regardless of card type."
- Rate review process: merchant shares 3-6 months transaction history → Shopify reviews → potentially lower rate for high GMV
- When to escalate: custom rate requests above standard Plus → Payments team

### Shop Pay
- 250M+ users, up to 50% better conversion vs guest checkout
- Only works when Shopify Payments is enabled as primary processor
- 12% of US ecommerce powered by Shopify Checkout

### Third-party PSP coexistence
- Merchants CAN use Shopify Payments alongside Adyen, Stripe, Mollie etc.
- Without Shopify Payments as primary: additional transaction fee applies (0.5-2% by plan)
- Fee waived when Shopify Payments is primary processor
- Best-of-breed framing: "Shopify for commerce, Adyen for enterprise payments — they coexist"

### Country availability
- 23 countries: US, CA, UK, IE, AU, NZ, DE, AT, BE, NL, DK, SE, FI, IT, ES, FR, PT, SG, HK, JP, CZ, RO, CH
- Country-specific payment methods: iDEAL (NL), Bancontact (BE), Afterpay (AU), etc.
- NF5 compliance France: resolved as of 2025 — address proactively if raised

### Key selling points
- PCI DSS Level 1 compliant — merchant doesn't need their own certification
- One less vendor: billing, disputes, payouts all in Shopify admin
- Unified reporting: payment data + order data in one place
- Multi-currency: accept in 130+ currencies, settle in store currency

### Escalation triggers
- Custom rate requests above standard Plus rates → Payments team
- Interchange++ pricing requests → Payments team
- PSD2/SCA compliance questions → Payments compliance team
- Country-specific regulatory questions (NF5, PSD2 exemptions) → legal/compliance
