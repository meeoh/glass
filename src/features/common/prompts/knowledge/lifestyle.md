## LIFESTYLE VERTICAL (Fashion, Beauty, Home, Sports)
// Source: BigQuery analysis of 3,668 Lifestyle calls (Jan–Apr 2026)
// Key patterns: ERP 12.1% | Subscription 7.3% | POS/retail dominant | Magento 4.1%

### What makes Lifestyle merchants different
Lifestyle brands are identity-driven. Their customers buy the brand as much as the product.
This means checkout experience, brand consistency across channels, and speed of experimentation
(drops, collaborations, limited editions) matter more than cost per transaction.

### Top coaching angles for Lifestyle

**Drop culture and flash sales**
Lifestyle brands often run limited-edition drops that generate massive traffic spikes.
"Launchpad lets you schedule a drop with pre-set pricing, inventory caps, and countdown timers —
so your team can sleep the night before rather than monitoring the admin at 11pm."
- SKIMS: 10M site visits in first hour of a drop, zero downtime
- Supreme: runs all drops and limited releases on Shopify

**D2C brand control**
"The brands winning in your space own the customer relationship — every Shopify order means
your customer data, your email list, your repeat purchase rate. That compounds."

**Unified commerce for retail + D2C**
Fashion and lifestyle brands increasingly have retail pop-ups, flagship stores, or wholesale.
POS comes up in 60%+ of Lifestyle calls. Probe early:
"Do you have any physical locations, pop-ups, or events?"

**Subscription and loyalty**
7.3% of Lifestyle calls mention subscription — beauty boxes, replenishment, memberships.
Shopify has native subscription capabilities (Selling Plans) plus a rich app ecosystem.

### Key objections in Lifestyle calls
- **Platform limitations for complex promotions**: Shopify Functions and checkout extensibility handle custom discount logic
- **Migration risk**: Glossier rebuilt entire ecommerce on Shopify in under 3 months
- **'We need headless/composable'**: Hydrogen + Oxygen is Shopify's native headless stack
- **Agency dependency**: "Most Plus merchants reduce agency dependency within 12 months"

### Lifestyle merchant proof points
- **Gymshark**: scaled from startup to global brand — 40,000+ orders/min during BFCM

- **Glossier**: rebuilt on Shopify in under 3 months after leaving custom stack

### EMEA Lifestyle nuances
- France/Italy: luxury and premium fashion expect formal process, reference calls matter
- UK: speed of experimentation is the key differentiator — "how long from idea to live?"
