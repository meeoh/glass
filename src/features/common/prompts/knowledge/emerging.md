## EMERGING VERTICAL (High-Growth D2C Brands, Digital-Native, Venture-Backed)
// Source: BigQuery analysis of 827 Emerging calls (Jan–Apr 2026)
// Key patterns: ERP 19.6% | Pricing 36% | Subscription 10.8% | B2B 5.3%

### What makes Emerging brands different
Emerging brands are high-growth, often digital-native, sometimes venture-backed.
They're building infrastructure as they scale — not replacing legacy systems, but 
choosing the right foundation for the next 5 years.

Common profiles: D2C startups scaling fast, brand-new categories, social commerce brands,
creator economy brands, subscription-first businesses, global-from-day-one brands.

### ERP is infrastructure they're building — not replacing
ERP comes up in 19.6% of Emerging calls — surprisingly high for young brands.
This is because fast-growing brands hit ERP complexity sooner than expected as they scale.

ERP is NOT a competitor. It's infrastructure they're growing into.
"As you scale, you'll need a commerce layer that connects cleanly to your back office 
when you get there. Shopify has native connectors to NetSuite, Dynamics 365, and SAP — 
so you're not locked into a platform that creates integration debt later."

For Emerging brands, the ERP conversation is about future-proofing:
"What does your order management and inventory look like today, and how are you thinking 
about that as you grow to 10x your current volume?"

### Speed and experimentation are the killer arguments
Emerging brands move fast. The #1 differentiator for this vertical isn't cost — it's velocity.

"How long does it take your team from idea to live A/B test? Shopify merchants do it in hours, 
not weeks. At your growth stage, 50 experiments per year vs 5 is a genuine competitive moat."

Other speed arguments:
- Shopify ships 300+ features per year — your platform keeps up as commerce evolves
- Launchpad for product drops without engineering work
- App Store for adding capabilities without custom dev
- Checkout extensibility for custom experiences without touching the core

### D2C-first, then omnichannel
Emerging brands often start pure D2C, then add channels as they grow:
- Retail pop-ups and markets → Shopify POS, unified inventory
- Wholesale to boutiques → B2B on Shopify, company accounts
- International → Shopify Markets, multi-currency, duties/taxes

"Start with D2C on Shopify, and when you're ready for retail, B2B, or international — 
it's all already built into the platform. You don't rebuild, you expand."

### Subscription is 10.8% of Emerging calls
High-growth D2C brands often have subscription components — replenishment, boxes, memberships.
"Shopify has native subscription capability through Selling Plans, plus the world's largest 
subscription app ecosystem (Recharge, Bold, Skio). Customers can pause, skip, swap — 
all self-serve."

### The "we'll outgrow Shopify" objection
Some Emerging brands worry that Shopify is for SMBs and they'll need to migrate later.
This is one of the most important objections to address:

"The brands that stayed on Shopify from day one are the ones winning at scale — Gymshark 
scaled from startup to $500M+ on Shopify. SKIMS launched with 10M site visits in the first 
hour with zero downtime. Glossier rebuilt their entire ecommerce in 3 months after leaving 
a custom stack. The question isn't whether Shopify scales — it's whether your team is ready 
to move as fast as Shopify can."

### Key objections in Emerging calls
- **"We're too early for Shopify"** → "The best time to build on Shopify is before you 
  have legacy debt. Every brand that migrated wishes they'd started here."
- **"We need custom everything"** → "What specifically needs to be custom? Most 'custom' 
  requirements are already in the App Store or solved with Shopify Functions."
- **"We might outgrow it"** → see above — Gymshark, SKIMS, Glossier are the answer
- **"We're venture-backed and need enterprise features"** → Shopify Plus includes all 
  enterprise features; many VC-backed brands use Plus from Series A onwards
- **"We need to integrate with [ERP] eventually"** → "Shopify has native connectors for 
  NetSuite, Dynamics 365, and SAP. You're not building integration debt."

### Emerging merchant proof points

- **SKIMS**: 10M site visits in first hour of a drop, zero downtime
- **Glossier**: rebuilt on Shopify in under 3 months after leaving custom stack
- **Princess Polly (AU)**: fast fashion D2C scaled globally on Shopify
- **boAt (India)**: electronics brand scaled to market leader on Shopify

### What to ask Emerging brands
- "What does your release cycle look like — how long from idea to live feature?"
- "What's the one thing your current platform can't do that's blocking growth?"
- "When you think about where you want to be in 3 years, what does your commerce 
  infrastructure need to look like?"
- "Have you mapped out what a migration would look like if you outgrow your current platform?"
  (Usually they haven't — this surfaces the hidden risk of their current choice)
