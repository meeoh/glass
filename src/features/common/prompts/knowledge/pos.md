## SHOPIFY POS — PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

### What it is
- Point-of-sale for physical retail: in-store, pop-ups, markets, events
- Runs on iPad (primary) and iPhone — no proprietary hardware required
- Unified with online store: one inventory, one customer record, one admin
- Included with every Shopify plan; POS Pro features require POS Pro subscription (or included with Plus)

### The core pitch: unified commerce
- One inventory: sell in-store → online stock updates instantly. Oversell is eliminated.
- One customer record: customer buys online, returns in-store — staff sees full purchase history
- One analytics dashboard: in-store + online revenue in the same report
- Single MID across all locations (vs separate MIDs per location with legacy POS = monthly reconciliation nightmare)

### MID consolidation — the closer
Legacy POS: each store location has its own Merchant ID and often its own bank account. For a 10-store retailer: 10 bank accounts, 10 MIDs, manual reconciliation every month.
Shopify POS: one MID, one bank account, one payout across all locations.
QUESTION TO ASK: "How many separate MIDs and bank accounts do you reconcile across your stores today?"

### Network effect
"When a customer taps their card in-store, if they've ever shopped on ANY Shopify store before — not just yours — their profile populates automatically. 250M+ Shop Pay users in the network. Your new in-store customer might already be in the Shopify ecosystem. No standalone POS can do that."

### Hardware
- Card readers: Shopify Tap & Chip Reader (Bluetooth), Shopify POS Terminal (countertop)
- Accessories: receipt printers (Star Micronics), barcode scanners, cash drawers
- No proprietary lock-in — standard Bluetooth peripherals work
- Hardware available through Shopify Hardware Store

### Location setup
- Each physical location = a "Location" in Shopify admin
- Inventory tracked per location, transfers managed in admin
- Up to 200 locations on Plus (competitors cap at 5-15)

### Competitive positioning
- vs Square: "Square is great for simple retail. The gap at your scale: Square and your ecommerce are two separate customer records unless you've built custom middleware."
- vs Lightspeed: "Lightspeed is a strong POS. Are your in-store and online customers the same person in your system, or two separate records?"
- vs Retail Pro / Sin 7: "Retail Pro works as a standalone POS. The issue is joining in-store and online customer data. How are you doing that today?"
- vs Oracle MICROS: "Oracle is enterprise-grade POS. The gap: what does your middleware connecting Oracle to your online store cost annually?"

### Plus inclusion
"When you license Shopify Plus, POS is included in the licence — not a separate purchase."
