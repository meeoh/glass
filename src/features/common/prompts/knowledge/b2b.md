## B2B ON SHOPIFY — PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

### What it is
- Native B2B commerce built into Shopify — not a separate platform or plugin
- Available on Plus (full feature set) and Advanced (limited)
- B2B and D2C run from the same Shopify admin — one product catalog, one inventory

### Core capabilities
- Company accounts: create company profiles with multiple locations and buyers
- Custom catalogs: different product assortments and pricing per company
- Volume pricing: quantity-based price breaks (buy 100+, get 15% off)
- Payment terms: Net 15, Net 30, Net 60 — invoice-based with automatic reminders
- Partial payments and deposits: collect a % upfront, balance on delivery
- Draft orders: sales reps create orders on behalf of customers
- Quick order list: buyers order by SKU in bulk (CSV upload or paste)
- Tax-exempt purchasing, PO number attachment, approval workflows

### The "same store" advantage
- ONE product catalog, ONE inventory — B2B and D2C share the same source of truth
- No middleware synchronization between a B2B platform and D2C platform
- Analytics: B2B and D2C revenue in the same dashboard
- Customer who starts as B2B and later buys D2C = same record

### ERP integration
- Native integrations: NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP Business One
- All other ERPs: API-based via Shopify Admin API
- Common pattern: ERP handles fulfillment + accounting, Shopify handles commerce + customer experience
- QUESTION TO ASK: "What's your current ERP, and how does your B2B commerce system talk to it today?"

### Competitive positioning
- vs OroCommerce / BigCommerce B2B: "Those are B2B-only. If your buyers also shop D2C — or you want one platform — Shopify handles both without middleware."
- vs Magento B2B: "Magento's B2B module is self-hosted — your team owns the infrastructure. Shopify's B2B is native SaaS — no patches, no hosting, no DevOps."
- vs custom-built portals: "Custom portals work until you need to add a feature. Payment terms, volume pricing, company accounts are built-in and maintained by Shopify."

### Key diagnostic questions
- "Do you have a wholesale or trade channel today? How do those buyers access your catalog and place orders?"
- "Are your B2B customers on a separate system from your D2C customers — or is it one record?"
- "What payment terms do your B2B customers expect? Net 30? Do you manage that manually today?"

### Escalation triggers
- Complex pricing rules (matrix pricing, negotiated per-SKU) → solutions engineering
- EDI/punchout catalog requirements → solutions engineering
- SAP or Oracle ERP integration → solutions engineering
- Multi-entity B2B (different legal entities selling to different buyer groups) → solutions engineering
