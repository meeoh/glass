## SHOPIFY PLUS — PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE

### What Plus unlocks (vs Advanced)
- 9 Expansion Stores: separate storefronts for international, retail, wholesale, B2B — one contract
- Unlimited staff accounts (Advanced caps at 15)
- 200 inventory locations (Advanced caps at 10)
- Shopify Flow: automation — trigger/condition/action workflows for ops, marketing, fraud
- Launchpad: schedule product drops, flash sales, theme changes with real-time analytics
- Shopify Functions: custom checkout logic, shipping rules, discount rules
- Checkout extensibility: customize checkout (payment method visibility, upsells, custom fields)
- B2B on Shopify: full feature set (company accounts, catalogs, payment terms)
- 25 Hydrogen storefronts with free Oxygen hosting (headless/composable)
- Dedicated Merchant Success Manager + 24/7 priority phone support

### Upgrade trigger signals
- GMV approaching or exceeding $1M/year → Plus pays for itself in checkout conversion + lower transaction fees
- International expansion: need separate storefronts → Expansion Stores
- B2B launch: payment terms, company accounts, custom catalogs → only on Plus
- Automation complexity: manually processing orders, tagging customers → Flow automates this
- Agency dependency: paying agency for things Shopify handles natively → Plus reduces this
- Team growth: hitting the 15-staff cap on Advanced
- Peak season: BFCM/flash sales need Launchpad scheduling

### ROI conversation
- Transaction fee savings: Plus rate lower than Advanced. Calculate savings at their GMV.
- Checkout conversion: 15% better than competitors average — at their volume, what's 15% more completions worth?
- Agency cost reduction: most Plus merchants reduce agency dependency within 12 months
- QUESTION TO ASK: "What does your team spend the most time on manually? What would it mean to automate that?"

### Common "happy on Advanced" objections
- "We don't need all those features": "Which of these does your business NOT need: international expansion, B2B, automation, or unlimited staff? Most merchants start with one and end up using three within 18 months."
- "The price increase isn't justified": "Let's run the numbers. What's your monthly GMV? At that volume, the transaction fee difference alone often covers the plan upgrade."
- "We'll upgrade when we're ready": "The merchants who get the most from Plus are the ones who have it before they need it — not after."
