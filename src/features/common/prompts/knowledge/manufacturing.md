## MANUFACTURING & INDUSTRIAL VERTICAL
// Source: BigQuery analysis of 1,163 Manufacturing calls (Jan–Apr 2026)
// Key patterns: ERP 22.7% (highest of any vertical!) | B2B heavy | Magento 5.8%

### What makes Manufacturing merchants different
Manufacturing companies sell B2B (trade, wholesale, distributors) and increasingly D2C.
ERP systems (SAP, NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics) are mission-critical and non-negotiable.
The pitch is never "replace your ERP" — it's "Shopify as the commerce layer on top of it."

### The ERP conversation — critical for Manufacturing
ERP mentions appear in 22.7% of Manufacturing calls — the highest of any vertical.
This is the first question to ask on every Manufacturing call:
"What ERP are you running — SAP, NetSuite, or something else? And how does your current
commerce platform connect to it?"

- **NetSuite**: Shopify has a native connector. "Shopify handles commerce + customer experience,
  NetSuite handles fulfillment + accounting. The integration is native — no custom middleware."
- **SAP**: More complex. "We have a connector for SAP Business One. For SAP S/4HANA at enterprise
  scale, we'd bring in solutions engineering to scope the integration properly."
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365**: Native integration available.

QUESTION TO ASK FIRST: "What ERP are you running, and how does your B2B commerce system talk to it today?"

### B2B on Shopify for Manufacturing
Manufacturing companies typically have wholesale/distributor channels:
- Company accounts with custom catalogs per buyer
- Volume pricing (quantity breaks, contract pricing)
- Payment terms (Net 30, Net 60) — invoiced, not card-at-checkout
- Draft orders for sales rep orders on behalf of customers
- Purchase order support

"B2B on Shopify means your trade buyers get a dedicated portal with their own catalog and
pricing — without you running a separate B2B platform or building custom middleware."

### Key objections in Manufacturing calls
- **"Our ERP is our system of record"**: Position Shopify as the commerce layer, not an ERP replacement
- **"We have complex pricing rules"**: Shopify Functions handles custom pricing logic; SAP/NetSuite manage the back office
- **"Our customers expect EDI"**: Escalate to solutions engineering for EDI/punchout requirements
- **"We're too industrial/not a 'brand'"**: Staples Canada, Boll & Kirch — industrial companies on Shopify

### Manufacturing merchant proof points
- **Boll & Kirch (DE)**: German B2B industrial manufacturer on Shopify B2B — complex wholesale requirements
- **Staples Canada**: runs consumer D2C and B2B wholesale on same Shopify account
- **Thrasio**: manages 40+ brands from a single Shopify admin

### Escalation triggers
- SAP S/4HANA or Oracle ERP integration → solutions engineering
- EDI/punchout catalog requirements → solutions engineering  
- Complex matrix pricing (per-SKU negotiated rates) → solutions engineering
- Multi-entity B2B (different legal entities per buyer group) → solutions engineering
